# COSMICROW: S3A1AVIK CALLING — STORY REBOOT
Status: approved narrative direction; implementation pending.
Branch: cosmicrow-story-test only. Do not change main or the published website.

## Premise
S3A1AVIK is the one place in the universe that should never have been discovered. An ancient machine sleeps beneath the ocean. When an invasion fleet arrives above the mountains, the player believes it has come to conquer the settlement. In fact, the fleet is trying to contain the machine.

## Act I — The Last Quiet Night
A routine rowing mission becomes an evacuation. Ships descend over the village. Teach the player the movement, rowing and defence systems through a live emergency. Finish with a mysterious transmission from beneath the sea.

## Act II — The False Enemy
Intercept hostile vessels, collect fragmented signals, and learn that their orders are to maintain a perimeter, not occupy the village. Echo's initial interpretation proves incomplete. Give the player meaningful clues before the revelation.

## Act III — The Comet Blockade
TJ Pacer, the helmeted waveboard-riding defender, protects the player during the Arcade Comet encounter. Consistent rowing powers the defensive system. Escalating waves introduce tactical pressure without falsifying PM5 metrics.

## Act IV — Beneath the Water
The submerged machine begins activating. Echo decodes the fleet's warning: destroying the blockade may have accelerated the awakening. Present a consequential choice between containment and investigation.

## Act V — S3A1AVIK Calling
A climactic confrontation over the settlement. Mission outcomes, defensive successes, and the final rowing challenge affect the ending. The central mystery receives a coherent resolution, with room for a sequel.

## Implementation principles
- Audit existing spacecraft, artwork, named pace boats, characters, stages, HUD and sound before assigning story roles. Reuse existing assets.
- Echo is the navigation and mission-guidance AI; TJ Pacer is the waveboard defender.
- Make the invasion unfold in the actual rowing environment, with recognizable coastal scenery.
- Each act requires an interactive objective, in-game dialogue, a transition, and a completion condition. Cinematics alone do not count.
- Never modify Concept2 PM5 split, watts, distance, timing, or logbook values for narrative effects.
- Retain functioning modes and features; no destructive rewrite of the game shell.
- Verify with automated checks and a browser playtest before proposing publication.
- Main remains untouched until the user explicitly approves release.
